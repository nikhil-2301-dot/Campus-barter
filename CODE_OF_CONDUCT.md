# Code of Conduct

We want Campus Barter to be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their college, year, background or experience level.

## What we expect
- Be kind and patient, especially with beginners
- Give feedback on the work, never attack the person
- Accept that people disagree, and discuss it calmly
- Respect other people's privacy

## What we do not accept
- Insults, harassment or threats
- Discrimination of any kind
- Sharing someone's private information without permission
- Spam or off-topic promotion

## What happens next
Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests that break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

## Reporting
If someone is breaking these rules, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or contact the maintainers through their GitHub profile.
